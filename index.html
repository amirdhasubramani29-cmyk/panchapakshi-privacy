Panchapakshi Daily Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Panchapakshi Daily respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Profile information entered by you.
  • Location (only if you allow it) to calculate sunrise and sunset.
  • App preferences stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

  • Calculate Panchapakshi schedules.
  • Provide personalized recommendations.
  • Improve the app experience.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Deletion

Panchapakshi Daily stores user profiles, preferences, and journal entries locally on your device. We do not store your personal data on our servers.

You may delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual family profiles within the app (where applicable).
  • Uninstalling the app.
  • Clearing the app's storage from your device settings, which permanently removes all locally stored data.
