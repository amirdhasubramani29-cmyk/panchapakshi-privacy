Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Panchapakshi Daily is built with privacy by design. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. All data related to your profiles, preferences, and daily usage is processed and stored locally on your device.

📋

Information We Collect

  • Profile information you enter (name, birth details for Panchapakshi calculations).
  • Location data — only if you grant permission — used exclusively to calculate accurate local sunrise and sunset times.
  • App preferences (theme, language, notification settings) stored locally on your device.
  • Journal entries and notes you create within the app, stored on-device only.
⚙️

How We Use Your Information

  • Calculate personalised Panchapakshi bird schedules and yama states for your day.
  • Determine accurate sunrise times based on your location for precise timing.
  • Send optional notifications when each yama state begins, if you enable them.
  • Improve app experience through anonymised, non-identifiable usage insights.
🤝

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers or analytics companies. Your profile and journal data never leaves your device.

✅

All personal data is stored exclusively on your device. Panchapakshi Daily has no backend server that receives or stores your personal information.

📍

App Permissions

  • Location (optional): Used only to calculate local sunrise time. Never stored beyond the session.
  • Notifications (optional): Used to send yama state alerts at the times you configure. You may disable this anytime in system settings.
📝

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

💾

Where Your Data Lives

Panchapakshi Daily stores all user data — including profiles, preferences, journal entries, and notification settings — locally on your device. We do not maintain any cloud database or server-side records of your personal information.

ℹ️

Because no data is sent to our servers, there is no account to delete and no server-side request needed. You have full control over your data at all times.

🗑️

How to Delete Your Data

You may delete your data at any time using any of the following methods:

  1. 1
    Delete individual profiles in-app: Go to the Profile section within Panchapakshi Daily and remove individual family member profiles. This deletes all associated data for that profile.
  2. 2
    Clear app storage: On Android, go to Settings → Apps → Panchapakshi Daily → Storage → Clear Data. On iOS, go to Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Panchapakshi Daily → Offload App. This permanently removes all locally stored data.
  3. 3
    Uninstall the app: Uninstalling Panchapakshi Daily removes all data associated with the app from your device.
⚠️

Data deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, your profiles, preferences, and journal entries cannot be recovered. Please export or note any important information before deleting.
